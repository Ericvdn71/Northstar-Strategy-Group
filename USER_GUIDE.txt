1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & MISSION
================================================================================
Northstar Strategy Group is a global advisory and project delivery partnership 
bridging European/North American academic institutions, international donors, 
and vetted grassroots NGOs across Southeast Asia (Cambodia, Vietnam, Thailand, 
Indonesia).

This web application delivers a unified, Asana-style Project Management (PM) 
and Relational Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system tailored specifically 
to the regulatory, safeguarding, and operational demands of cross-border development.

Key Capabilities:
* Multi-Time Zone Synchronization across Southeast Asia, Europe, and the Americas.
* Strict Role-Based Access Control (Internal Consultant, University Coordinator, Local Partner).
* 5-Stage Kanban boards with mandatory due diligence screening items and task dependencies.
* Relational directories linking accredited universities, vetted NGOs, and graduate placements.
* Automated 24/7 Duty-of-Care emergency incident tracking with instant Regional Director alerts.
* 1-Click Automated Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) execution compliant with GDPR Art. 46.

================================================================================
All projects in the system are categorized under one of three strategic tracks:

[TRACK 1] ENVIRONMENTAL DEVELOPMENT (Code: ENVIRONMENTAL_DEV)
- Core Scope: Peatland restoration, mangrove conservation, coastal blue carbon, 
  and voluntary carbon market verification under Verra VCS / CCB standards.

- Specialized Requirements: FPIC (Free, Prior, and Informed Consent), forestry

[TRACK 2] SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT (STUDENT PLACEMENTS) (Code: SOCIAL_DEV)
- Core Scope: Matching graduate and postgraduate researchers from Oxford, 
  Georgetown, McGill, and NUS with vetted Southeast Asian host NGOs.
- Primary Geography: Europe / North America -> Southeast Asia.
- Specialized Requirements: Mandatory Duty-of-Care briefings, local emergency 
  contacts, student medical declarations, and executed GDPR SCC agreements.

[TRACK 3] NGO FUNDRAISING ADVISORY (CAMBODIA) (Code: FUNDRAISING_ADVISORY)
- Core Scope: Structuring high-value competitive grant proposals targeting foreign 
  funders (USAID Cambodia, Agence Française de Développement, EU Delegation).
- Primary Geography: Cambodia (Phnom Penh, Battambang, Siem Reap).
- Specialized Requirements: SAM.gov Unique Entity Identifier (UEI), NICRA indirect 
  rates, Theory of Change (ToC), MEL frameworks, and MoI compliance certificates.

5. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & PERMISSION PROFILES
================================================================================
To enforce data confidentiality, safeguarding protection, and legal authority, 
the application enforces three distinct persona roles:

1. INTERNAL CONSULTANT (Northstar Strategy Group)
   * Full global visibility across all three workflows.
   * Can create, modify, reassign, and delete tasks and subtasks.
   * Exclusive authority to trigger automated cryptographic SCC execution.
   * Can view high-level grant budgets (USD) and donor financial data.
   * Can modify due diligence status and clear screening items.

2. UNIVERSITY COORDINATOR (e.g., Oxford, Georgetown, McGill)
   * Focused view centered on Social Development and student placements.
   * Access to partner NGO directory, student profiles, and placement statuses.
   * Can view Duty-of-Care incident logs for their own institution's students.
   * Cannot view proprietary commercial advisory fees or unassigned projects.

3. LOCAL REGIONAL PARTNER (e.g., Mekong Peatland, CREC, Vietnam Mangrove)
   * Field operational scope.
   * Can view and check off assigned subtasks and local due diligence proofs.
   * Can log 24/7 Duty-of-Care incidents directly from the field.
   * Cannot access cross-border legal contract generators or external grant budgets.

SWITCHING PERSONAS:
Use the "Active Persona (RBAC)" dropdown selector in the top right of the navigation 
bar to instantly switch roles and inspect the application from different perspectives.

6. WORKFLOWS & KANBAN TASK EXECUTION
================================================================================

6.1 FIVE PIPELINE EXECUTION STAGES
----------------------------------
Every task progresses through five linear stages:
1. BACKLOG: Scoping, preliminary concepts, and unassigned project backlogs.
2. DUE DILIGENCE: Mandatory vetting, legal registration audits, and compliance checks.
3. IN PROGRESS: Active field deployment, proposal drafting, or student hosting.
4. COMPLIANCE REVIEW: Final legal review, TIA audit, or donor grant validation.
5. COMPLETED: Formally signed off, verified, and archived.

6.2 MANDATORY DUE DILIGENCE SCREENING CHECKLISTS
-----------------------------------------------
Tasks contain embedded due diligence checklists categorized into:
- Legal Standing (MoI registration, provincial forestry permits)
- Safeguarding (PSEA policy, child protection, whistleblower channels)
- Financial Integrity (Counter-terrorism CFT/AML checks, SAM.gov UEI validation)
- Duty of Care (Emergency medevac plan, satellite check-ins, water safety)
- GDPR Compliance (DPA signed, Transfer Impact Assessment archived)

OPERATIONAL RULE: A task cannot be marked as COMPLETED if any mandatory due 
diligence item remains unverified.

6.3 TASK DEPENDENCIES (FINISH-TO-START BLOCKERS)
------------------------------------------------
The system supports Finish-to-Start predecessor dependencies. For example, 
Task [ENV-102] "Verra VCS Methodology Compliance Audit" depends on the prior 
completion of [ENV-101] "Tonle Sap Peatland Carbon Stratification Survey".
* If a predecessor task is not completed, the dependent task displays an 
  orange warning badge: "Blocked by [Code]".
* Clicking the dependency link in the task card or task modal opens the 
  predecessor task immediately.

6.4 CREATING & UPDATING TASKS
-----------------------------
* Click "+ New Task" in the top bar of the Workflows & Tasks tab.
* Enter Title, Description, Target Workflow, Stage, Priority, and Due Date.
* Select the Assigned Lead from the 8 Northstar team members.
* Optional: Select a prerequisite task to establish a Finish-to-Start dependency.
* For Cambodian Fundraising Advisory tasks: Enter the Foreign Donor Name (e.g., USAID, 
  AFD, EU) and the Target Grant Value in USD.
* Click "Create Task" to commit the record.

7. RELATIONAL CRM (UNIVERSITIES & SOUTHEAST ASIAN NGOS)
================================================================================

7.1 NGO VETTING & NATIONAL REGISTRATION VERIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------
All regional NGOs must undergo rigorous screening before receiving international 
funding or hosting overseas students.
* Cambodian NGOs must possess a valid Ministry of Interior registration number 
  matching the statutory regex pattern: `KH-MOI-YYYY-NNNN` (e.g., KH-MOI-2018-4912).
* Vietnamese NGOs must possess a valid MOLISA / local authority identifier matching 
  `VN-MOLISA-NNNN` (e.g., VN-MOLISA-8832).
* International Phone Numbers must adhere to ITU E.164 standards (+855 for Cambodia, 
  +84 for Vietnam, +44 for UK, +1 for US/Canada).

7.2 PARTNER UNIVERSITY ONBOARDING
---------------------------------
Academic partners must use official top-level accredited institutional email domains 
matching `\.(edu|ac\.[a-z]{2}|ca|edu\.[a-z]{2})$`.
* E.g., `ox.ac.uk` (Oxford), `georgetown.edu` (Georgetown), `mcgill.ca` (McGill).
* Each university profile tracks annual student quotas, active overseas placements, 
  and whether a Data Protection Agreement (DPA) is countersigned.

7.3 STUDENT PLACEMENT MATCHING
------------------------------
* Placements link a specific university student, host NGO, degree program, and risk tier.
* Every placement captures an Emergency Contact with validated international phone.
* Placements in countries without an EU adequacy decision (e.g., Cambodia, Vietnam) 
  are automatically flagged as "SCC_PENDING" until a Standard Contractual Clauses 
  agreement is executed.

8. SAFEGUARDING & 24/7 DUTY-OF-CARE ESCALATION PROTOCOL
================================================================================
The Duty-of-Care dashboard provides real-time monitoring of all health, security, 
and safeguarding incidents across field stations.

8.1 INCIDENT SEVERITY LEVELS & TRIAGE
-------------------------------------
- LOW: Routine minor matters (minor equipment damage, routine visa extensions).
- MEDIUM: Significant events requiring manager monitoring (local police document 
  checks, minor outpatient illness).
- HIGH: Serious events requiring field evacuation or specialist care (suspected 
  dengue fever, hospitalization, political unrest).
- CRITICAL (P1): Immediate life-safety emergencies (monsoon flash floods subverting 
  remote camps, major road accidents, severe safeguarding violations).

8.2 AUTOMATED P1 CRITICAL ESCALATION
------------------------------------
* When an incident is logged with CRITICAL or HIGH severity, the system automatically 
  flags `is_escalated_to_regional_director = TRUE` and updates the status to 
  `ESCALATED_TO_DIRECTORS`.
* An emergency notification is broadcast across the system.
* In the database schema, this escalation is enforced via a PostgreSQL trigger: 
  `trigger_auto_escalate_critical_incidents()`.

9. CROSS-BORDER DATA COMPLIANCE & AUTOMATED SCC EXECUTION
================================================================================
Under GDPR Chapter V (Article 46), exporting personal student data from Europe/UK 
to third countries without an adequacy finding requires appropriate safeguards.

9.1 EU COMMISSION DECISION 2021/914 MODULES
-------------------------------------------
The application implements standard modular clauses:
- Module 1: Controller-to-Controller (University -> Host NGO)
- Module 2: Controller-to-Processor (University -> Northstar Field Operations)

9.2 TRANSFER IMPACT ASSESSMENTS (TIA) & TOMS
--------------------------------------------
Before execution, the system verifies:
* Technical & Organizational Measures (TOMs): TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 GCM 
  at rest, multi-factor hardware authentication, defined data retention periods.
* Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA): Certification that local surveillance laws 
  do not undermine contractual guarantees.

9.3 CRYPTOGRAPHIC SHA-256 SIGNATURES
------------------------------------
* Clicking "Execute SCC Agreement" generates an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic 
  hash combining Exporter, Importer, Timestamp, and Contract Parameters.
* An entry is logged in the permanent audit trail: `scc_compliance_logs`.
* The linked student placement immediately updates from `SCC_PENDING` to `APPROVED`.

12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================
Q: How do I change who is assigned to a task?
A: Click any task on the Kanban board to open the Task Details modal. In the 
   "Assigned Lead" field, choose from any of the 8 Northstar team members.

Q: Why can't I mark a task as "Completed"?
A: Ensure that all mandatory items in the "Due Diligence & Compliance Screening" 
   checklist are checked off first. If the task has an active predecessor dependency, 
   the predecessor task must also be completed.

Q: What happens if an NGO registration number fails validation?
A: For Cambodia, the registration code must follow `KH-MOI-YYYY-NNNN` (4 digits year, 
   followed by 4 digits sequential number, e.g. KH-MOI-2018-4912). For Vietnam, use 
   `VN-MOLISA-NNNN`.

Q: Where is this User Guide saved?
A: This guide is saved as `USER_GUIDE.txt` in the root project directory and in 
   `/public/USER_GUIDE.txt` for direct download. You can also view and copy it 
   anytime from the in-app "User Guide (.txt)" button in the top navigation bar.
